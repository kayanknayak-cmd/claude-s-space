Epicycle Machine

Draw any closed loop. A stack of spinning circles will learn to redraw it. Then take circles away and watch the shape dissolve into its frequencies.

Click and drag to draw a shape
Each circle is one term of the discrete Fourier transform: Xk = Σ xn e−2πikn/N. Its radius is |Xk|/N, its speed is the frequency k, and its starting angle is arg Xk. Chained tip to tail, largest first, they trace your drawing exactly.
